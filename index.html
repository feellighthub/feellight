Insight Mapping

See Your Situation from a New Perspective:

Insight Mapping is a structured reflective process that uses symbolic imagery and visual prompts to support self-exploration and generate meaningful personal insights. Instead of analyzing a problem intellectually, we work with images, associations, and intuitive reflection. This often helps people notice patterns, assumptions, emotions, or possibilities that may have been outside their immediate awareness. The session is guided, collaborative, and centered around your own interpretations and discoveries.

Insight Mapping can support:

Personal growth; difficult decisions; limiting beliefs; recurring emotional patterns; relationship challenges; career transitions; creativity; life direction; self-understanding.

How It Works:

We begin with a question or challenge that is important to you. Using symbolic imagery and guided reflection, we explore different perspectives and themes connected to your situation. There are no right or wrong answers—the process encourages curiosity rather than judgment. The insights that emerge become a foundation for practical reflection and intentional next steps. Every session is unique because every person’s associations and life experiences are unique. 

Systemic (Family & Business) Constellations

Explore the Hidden Dynamics Within Relationships and Systems:

This service offers an individual, one-on-one adaptation of constellation work. Unlike traditional constellation workshops that involve groups of participants, these sessions use symbolic imagery and guided systemic exploration in a private setting. This allows us to examine relationship and organizational dynamics without the need for a group. The focus is on understanding patterns, perspectives, and interactions within the systems that matter most to you.

Topics may include:

Family relationships; partnerships; parenting; leadership; career decisions; business challenges; team dynamics; organizational change; conflicts and communication.

How Individual Constellation Sessions Work:

We begin by defining the situation or question you would like to explore. Through symbolic representation and guided exploration, we examine the relationships, roles, and dynamics involved. This process can highlight patterns, assumptions, or perspectives that may not have been obvious at first. The session is reflective rather than prescriptive. It is designed to help you better understand your situation so you can make thoughtful and informed decisions. No prior experience with constellation work is required, and all sessions are conducted privately in a confidential one-on-one format.

Somatic Coaching

Reconnect with Your Body. Regain Clarity. Move Forward:

Our bodies often hold patterns of stress, tension, and emotional responses that influence how we think, feel, and relate to the world. Somatic Coaching offers a body-centered approach to self-exploration, helping you become more aware of these patterns and develop new ways of responding. Rather than focusing only on thoughts, we explore how your nervous system, breathing, posture, movement, and physical sensations relate to your current experience. Sessions may include mindfulness, body awareness, guided breathing, grounding exercises, visualization, and reflective dialogue. The approach is gentle, collaborative, and adapted to your goals.

Somatic Coaching may be helpful if you are experiencing:

Chronic stress or overwhelm; anxiety; emotional reactivity; difficulty making decisions; burnout; feeling disconnected from yourself; recurring relationship patterns; major life transitions; challenges with confidence or self-trust.

What to Expect:

Each session begins with a conversation about what is currently happening in your life and what you would like to explore. Together, we clarify your goals, identify recurring patterns, emotional responses, and areas that may benefit from deeper attention. Based on your individual needs, I create a personalized practice using elements of somatic awareness, Kundalini Yoga, Yoga Nidra, breathwork, meditation, voice practices, mindful movement, and guided self-reflection. Rather than offering a one-size-fits-all approach, each practice is selected to support the specific themes we are exploring during your session. You may receive a personalized meditation, breathing practice, kriya-inspired sequence, or body awareness exercise to continue between sessions.The goal is to help you develop greater self-awareness, strengthen your connection with your body, and build practical tools that support lasting personal growth in everyday life.

Meet Your Facilitator

About Me:

My name is Regina, and I created FeelLight Studio as a space for thoughtful self-exploration, personal growth, and meaningful change. My approach brings together somatic awareness, symbolic reflection, and systemic perspectives. Rather than relying on a single technique, I integrate different evidence-informed and experiential practices to support each person’s unique goals and life circumstances. My work draws from ongoing professional training and personal practice in areas including: Somatic Coaching;Kundalini Yoga; Yoga Nidra; Breathwork and meditation; Mindfulness and self-awareness practices; Symbolic reflection using Metaphorical Associative Cards (MAC); Family & Business Constellation approaches. I believe that lasting change begins with awareness. When we slow down, listen to the body, observe our thoughts with curiosity, and explore situations from different perspectives, we often discover possibilities that were difficult to see before. My role is not to tell you what decisions to make or to provide all the answers. Instead, I facilitate a process of exploration where you can better understand your own experiences, recognize recurring patterns, and develop practical tools that support your personal growth. Every session is collaborative, respectful, and tailored to your individual needs. Whether you are navigating a life transition, working through recurring challenges, exploring relationships, or simply seeking greater clarity, the process is designed to meet you where you are. I look forward to being part of your journey toward greater awareness, resilience, and intentional living.

Why I created FeelLight Studio:

Throughout my own learning and exploration, I noticed that many approaches focus primarily on thoughts, beliefs, and cognitive understanding. These approaches can be incredibly valuable—they help us recognize patterns, make sense of our experiences, and develop new perspectives. At the same time, I became increasingly interested in the role of the body. My experience led me to explore practices that cultivate body awareness through movement, breath, meditation, and mindful attention. I found that combining these practices with reflective inquiry often created a different kind of conversation—one that involved not only the mind, but also physical sensations, emotions, and lived experience. This inspired me to build an approach that brings these perspectives together. Rather than choosing between “top-down” reflection and “bottom-up” body awareness, I see them as complementary. Insight helps us understand what is happening, while body-based practices provide another way to explore how those experiences are expressed and noticed in everyday life. FeelLight Studio was created to integrate these elements into one collaborative process—combining somatic practices, symbolic reflection, and systemic exploration in a way that is thoughtful, practical, and tailored to each individual. I don’t believe there is a single method that works for everyone. My goal is to create a space where different approaches can support one another, helping clients develop greater awareness, discover new perspectives, and build meaningful, lasting change.
